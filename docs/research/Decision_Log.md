## Decision log procedure

For each new decision:

1. Assign the next sequential ID.
2. Record the date and whether the decision is provisional, active, frozen, or retired.
3. Link the supporting development artifact.
4. State the exact condition under which reversal is permitted.
5. At protocol freeze, mark all analysis-affecting active decisions as frozen and record the source/configuration tag.
