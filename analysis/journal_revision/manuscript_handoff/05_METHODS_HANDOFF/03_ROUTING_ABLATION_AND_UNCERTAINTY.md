# Routing, ablation, repeated aggregation and statistics

Sources: INFERENCE_BEHAVIOR.md, journal_revision/experiments.py::run_ablation/run_panels, journal_revision/saved.py, journal_revision/statistics.py, journal_revision/verification.py, routing/inference.py, routing/policy_evaluator.py, evaluation/bootstrap.py and evaluation/comparisons.py.

All ablation systems share the same availability eligibility, selected panel, input records and training-fitted full-schema imputation. A/B use the original robust family; C/D use the original panel-selected family. A/C apply the original full-schema mapping to saved masked raw scores; B/D apply the saved panel mapping. B reproduces the historical robust policy and D the historical router. Calibration contrasts are B−A and D−C; family-switching contrasts C−A and D−B; D−A compares the complete framework with fixed imputation. Choices remain frozen across calibration arms; this does not evaluate a separately optimized selector without panel calibration.

Runtime chooses the largest fully observed prespecified panel, then its development-selected family/mapping. Excluded predictors are masked before applying training-fitted full-schema preprocessing. No supported panel produces availability abstention. Panel/family/calibrator assignments and larger-panel missing features are exported in 02_ROUTING_DISTRIBUTION.csv. A forced-panel stress estimate has a different input policy from natural runtime, even when sample sizes or rounded performance agree.

route_patient uses one loaded router. PIDD/Sylhet held-out evaluation averages one eligible OOF score in each of five repeats per record before calculating primary metrics. The research patient wrapper can average all 25 fold-router probabilities on a new record and apply additional input/range checks plus SHAP/report generation. That wrapper is a different execution mode, and its fold spread is not a confidence interval. It must not replace OOF evaluation predictions.

Per-repeat metrics are computed on each complete repeat. Their mean and sample SD (ddof=1) describe sensitivity across five dependent repeats. Primary metrics use participant-averaged probabilities. The two PIDD AUROC estimates use separate sources: forced core-metabolic masks all records to the four specified predictors; natural runtime selects the largest eligible panel. Their 2,000-resample percentile intervals use seeds 1700042 and 1600042 respectively.

All paired contrasts align records, outcomes and grouping before resampling. PIDD/BRFSS use outcome-stratified individual resampling; Sylhet samples complete identical-full-predictor-profile groups and Hospital130 complete patient clusters with replacement, retaining both classes. There are 2,000 resamples; intervals use the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. Literal effects are model_a minus model_b. Positive improvement equals that difference for AUROC/AP and its negative for Brier/log loss. Two-sided tail p is min(1, 2×min((count(delta≤0)+1)/(B+1), (count(delta≥0)+1)/(B+1))). Saved distributions preserve historical boundary arithmetic. Weighted metrics were checked against the reference implementation, including tied scores.

Holm families are separate: all prespecified panel contrasts within cohort/metric for the schema comparison (including the zero full control); five declared contrasts within cohort/metric for A/B/C/D; three original policy pairwise contrasts within cohort/metric for original Table S9. Family sizes are not inferred from only significant rows. The original all-five-model full-panel comparisons retain their own source family definitions. Bootstrap intervals condition on fitted predictions and do not include repeated refitting, retuning or independent selection uncertainty.

Confidence selection uses max(p,1−p). Grouped cohorts accept/reject complete groups ranked by mean confidence; individual cohorts rank individuals. Original target coverage, stable ordering and tie handling are retained. Coverage targets refer to the complete evaluation population; unsupported records cannot become accepted. Observation coverage, positive-event coverage, accepted/rejected prevalence and both availability/confidence rejection counts must accompany accepted-case metrics.
