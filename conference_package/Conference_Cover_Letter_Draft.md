Dear Program Chairs,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Availability-Aware Diabetes Screening Under
Incomplete Predictor Panels: A Multi-Environment Study,”** for presentation at
[conference name].

The work addresses a practical reliability gap in public-dataset diabetes
modelling. Instead of assuming that every predictor is always available or
pooling incompatible cohorts, we evaluate calibrated prediction across
prespecified within-dataset feature panels. The system retains endpoint
boundaries, compares routing with strong fixed-model baselines on identical
held-out participants, quantifies uncertainty and clinical utility, and abstains
when no evaluated route supports a request. The study also reports negative
algorithm-switching results, positive-event coverage during selective
prediction, subgroup acceptance, and missingness stress.

The manuscript's central claim is deliberately limited. It does not present a
universal diabetes model, future-incidence prediction, cross-cohort probability
fusion, or a clinically deployable system. Its contribution is an auditable
evaluation and software framework for reliability under incomplete predictor
availability across separately analysed diabetes-status environments.
